About Landmark Technologies

We are a DevOps training company on a mission to produce world-class cloud engineers through real-world, project-based learning.

Our Story

Founded in Atlanta, GA, Landmark Technologies was born from a simple belief: the best way to learn cloud engineering is to build real systems. Our e-Degree Master Program has trained hundreds of engineers who now work at top tech companies worldwide.

Our Approach

Every module in our curriculum is built around a real application — this Employee Directory app. Students deploy it on EC2, containerize it with Docker, orchestrate it on EKS, and automate it with CI/CD pipelines.

Our Community

Join a global community of DevOps engineers, cloud architects, and SREs. Our alumni network spans 30+ countries and includes engineers at AWS, Google, Microsoft, and hundreds of startups.

Our Mission

To bridge the gap between theoretical knowledge and real-world cloud engineering by delivering hands-on, project-based training that prepares engineers for the demands of modern software infrastructure.
